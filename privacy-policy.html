Wanderspace Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026
Developer: Graphicx Labs (sole proprietor), Thousand Oaks, California, USA
Contact: graphicxlabs@gmail.com

Wanderspace is a dashboard for a van, RV, boat, cabin or any converted space. It shows and controls the equipment you already own, whatever the brand. This page says what the app does with information, in plain words.

The short version

  • Wanderspace has no accounts. You never sign up or log in to us.
  • Wanderspace sends nothing about you to Graphicx Labs. We have no servers that receive your data, no analytics and no crash reporting.
  • Everything you enter stays on your device.
  • Your location is used only to get the weather for where you are, and only if you allow it.

What the app uses, and why

Your equipment. The app talks directly to the equipment you connect: battery monitors, solar controllers, inverters, tank sensors, light controllers, cameras and similar devices, over your own Wi-Fi network or Bluetooth. The addresses, passwords and keys you enter are stored on your device and used only to reach that equipment. They are never sent to us. Traffic between the app and your equipment stays on your network, or goes directly between your device and the equipment.

Outside services you connect. Some equipment makers offer an online service for history and remote access, and some weather or routing services need a subscription of your own. If you choose to enter an access token or login for such a service, the app uses it only to fetch your data from that service, and that service's privacy policy applies. We do not see the token or the data.

Location. If you allow it, the app reads your device's location to show the weather, forecast and alerts for where you are parked. To do that it sends your coordinates to a public weather service and to a place-name service so it can show the name of the town. If you type a town instead, that name is sent to a place search. These services are not run by us, and we do not receive or store your location. You can refuse the location permission and type a town instead, or leave the weather panel off.

Cameras and recordings. Camera video is viewed from your own cameras on your own network. Any recordings the app saves are stored on your device only.

Security PINs. The Security panel can ask for a PIN before showing cameras or opening doors. The names and PINs you set up are stored only on your device, as scrambled hashes, never the digits. They are never sent to us. They leave your device only inside a settings export you make yourself.

Microphone. Used only when you press the talk button to speak through a camera's speaker, or when the lights' music mode listens to the room. Audio is not recorded by the app and is not sent to us.

Notification access. If you grant it, the app can show and control what other apps are playing (the Music panel). It reads only media playback information. It does not read the content of your notifications and sends nothing off the device.

Bluetooth and nearby devices. Used to find and talk to your equipment. Not used for location.

The hub. If you set one device as the hub, it serves the dashboard to your other devices on the same local network. That traffic stays on your network.

Settings export. The Export button in Settings writes a file containing everything the app remembers, including your equipment passwords and keys. Keep that file private. It goes only where you send it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect or store personal information.
  • We do not sell or share data; there is none to share.
  • We do not use advertising or tracking.
  • We do not knowingly provide the app to children under 18, and it is not designed for them.

Deleting your data

Everything the app holds is on your device. Uninstalling Wanderspace removes it all. There is nothing held by us to delete. If you believe we hold anything about you, write to graphicxlabs@gmail.com and we will answer within 30 days.

Purchases

This version has no purchases. If a paid tier is added, payment is handled by Google Play, and Google's privacy policy covers the transaction. We would receive only what Google provides to confirm the purchase.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted at this address with a new effective date. Material changes are also noted in the app's release notes.

Your rights, wherever you are

Wanderspace is available worldwide. Privacy laws such as the EU and UK GDPR, Canada's PIPEDA, Australia's Privacy Act and California's CCPA give you rights to know what personal information a business holds about you, to correct it, to have it deleted, and to object to its use. Because Wanderspace collects no personal information and we hold nothing about you, every one of those rights is satisfied by default. We do not sell personal information and we do not transfer it across borders, because we have none. If you believe otherwise, or have any question, write to graphicxlabs@gmail.com and we will answer within 30 days.
